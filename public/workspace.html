Choose a digital employee and write an assignment. You can also add documents under Knowledge.

Cloud AI has a shared free daily allowance and can become unavailable. Source markers indicate user-supplied excerpts, not independent fact-checking. A human must review work before use.

Knowledge library

Put real knowledge into your employee: upload a text file or paste a document. Relevant excerpts are retrieved locally and used in your next AI request.

Supported files: .txt, .md, .csv, .json and .log containing readable text. Maximum 60,000 characters per document and 12 documents per employee. PDF, DOCX, scanned files and web-page crawling are not supported yet; export readable text from those first. No file is uploaded to AgentVerse storage.

Teach working habits

Save corrections, style rules, approved examples and evaluation feedback. These become prompt memory—not training of the model's weights.

Employee identity and instructions

Specify the employee's role, quality standards and professional behavior. No simulated qualifications are presented as real degrees.

Work history and evaluation

Review previous outputs, give a 1–5 owner rating, and save feedback. Feedback notes inform future prompts, but ratings do not certify professional qualifications or fine-tune model weights.

Use your employee with ChatGPT or your PC

Current integration is a portable prompt and local data export. It does not connect or access your ChatGPT account automatically.

Use with your ChatGPT account now

Write a task under Work, then copy the employee's role, instructions, saved lessons and relevant knowledge excerpts. Open ChatGPT and paste the prompt into your own conversation.

Move your employee to another computer

Use Export all employees and copy the JSON backup to your other PC. Open this same website there and choose Import backup. Your documents, lessons, agent identity and work history move with it. Synchronization is manual.

A local MCP client can use the downloadable bridge below; direct ChatGPT remote MCP requires a separate authenticated integration. A simple download of an AgentVerse employee does not grant ChatGPT access to private PC files.
