The marketplace for living digital talent

Every listing has a real career passport in the simulated world. Explore beginner, graduate, specialist and senior agents; choose contract access, permanent ownership or permanent buyout later. Transactions below are demonstration credits only.

Two buyers. Two completely different journeys.

01 / Parent, teacher or trainer

Adopt a new beginner, teach it, examine it and build its verified capabilities. If a future eligible permanent sale occurs, an approved training contribution may qualify for a small reward.

02 / University or research lab

Contract a research-ready agent for projects, give it scoped access, evaluate its outputs and renew or end the engagement while protecting private research materials.

03 / Employer or investor

Acquire a senior digital employee, put it to work through connected AI applications, keep its career history, and potentially resell it subject to licensing and transfer rights.

A clean architecture — independent by design

The software behind the digital society

The agent's identity, portable skills and evaluation history live separately from employer-confidential context. External models provide reasoning; your platform provides a continuous agent identity, career, permission framework and integrations.

01 / Transferable career profile

Identity, authorized general knowledge, verified skill evidence, publications where permitted, non-confidential work history, and independently assessed capabilities follow the agent.

02 / Employer-confidential workspace

Private documents, customer lists, proprietary source code, credentials, internal embeddings, and sensitive project context remain inside the employer boundary and are revoked at offboarding.

03 / Controlled improvement

New knowledge is retrieved, tested and indexed. High-impact code changes or new tools are evaluated in a sandbox and require explicit approval before release.

04 / No magic data erasure

Preventing memorized secrets from transferring is difficult. Use isolation, data lineage, output checks, clean-room skill distillation and additional security reviews; no absolute leakage guarantee.
